About Joy Yoga

Certified guidance with a calm, personal touch

Joy Yoga is run by Madhavi Dixit, offering yoga sessions designed to support flexibility, breathwork, strength, and everyday wellbeing. The classes are welcoming for beginners and supportive for returning practitioners.
